Privacy Policy

Hermes Home · Last updated September 27, 2026

Hermes Home is a private assistant used by a single household on its own computer. Only members of that household connect their accounts to it.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): to find bills, statements, payment confirmations and emails that need a reply. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar: to list the household's calendars and events, and to add reminder events such as bill due dates. It does not edit or delete existing events.

Where data goes

  • Data is processed and stored only on the household's own computer.
  • It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.
  • It is not used to train AI models. Email and calendar content is processed by an AI model that runs locally on the same computer.

Google API data

Hermes Home's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted by the household when no longer needed.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on the Google sign-in consent screen for Hermes Home.
